## Exact verbal consent script

LODEX staff uses the following script before enrolling a customer by phone or in person:

> May LODEX text you about your service inquiry, estimates, appointments, and project updates? Message frequency varies with your project. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for assistance or STOP to opt out anytime. Our Privacy Policy is at lodex.work/privacy and our Terms are at lodex.work/terms. Consent is optional and is not required to purchase services. Do you agree to receive these texts?

The customer is enrolled only after an explicit yes. LODEX records the consent date, method, and mobile number.
